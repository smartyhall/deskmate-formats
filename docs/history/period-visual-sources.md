# Period images and artifact sources for the DeskMate timeline

*Editorial sourcing record, 21 November 2026; source identities and crop preparation added 27 November 2026. Candidate historical images are not yet approved website assets.*

The timeline should show **how DeskMate was marketed, packaged, and supplied**, not only modern captures of the software. Prioritize original Tandy and Radio Shack catalog scans and contemporary magazine advertisements. For artifact photographs, retain credit for the modern photographer or collection. Never imply a modern photograph is itself a period image, or that a picture of an original disk verifies its contents.

## Selected illustrations for the first edition

**Editorial selection approved by the operator on 2026-11-21:** Choose a compact visual narrative rather than exhausting the source archive. These are the **four designated figures** for the initial historical timeline. Captions and alternative text below are the reviewed editorial wording; the machine-readable crop specification, dimensions, digests, and rights status live in [`history-figure-sources.json`](history-figure-sources.json). Do not hotlink ephemeral seller images, and do not claim the images are already published.

1. **The computer with software included — 1988 Tandy 1000 HX catalog page.** Source: [1988 Tandy Computer Catalog, printed and PDF p. 9](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Catalogs/Radio%20Shack/Tandy%20Computer%20Catalog%20and%20Software%20Reference%20Guide%20%281988%29%28Tandy%29.pdf#page=9). **Placement:** alongside the Personal DeskMate 2 / HX/TX passage. **Treatment:** the price block, the MS-DOS-in-ROM bullet, the included-Personal-DeskMate 2 bullet, and the introductory paragraph, cropped from the upper left of the leaf (`history.hx-1988-offer`). The product photograph and the specifications block are omitted. **Proposed caption:** “The 1988 Tandy catalog advertised the 1000 HX with Personal DeskMate 2 included. Its built-in ROM contained MS-DOS, not an explicitly advertised copy of DeskMate.” **Alt text:** “1988 Tandy 1000 HX catalog listing with a $699 price, feature bullets for MS-DOS in ROM and included Personal DeskMate 2, and introductory text.” **Status:** original catalog page visually inspected; crop prepared for private review; publication pending the rights decision in [the rights assessment](figure-rights-assessment.md).

2. **The range of DeskMate editions — 1988 software catalog listings.** Source: [1988 Tandy Computer Catalog, printed and PDF pp. 102–103](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Catalogs/Radio%20Shack/Tandy%20Computer%20Catalog%20and%20Software%20Reference%20Guide%20%281988%29%28Tandy%29.pdf#page=102). **Placement:** at the transition from the early bundled products to DeskMate 3, paired with a note on independently marketed software. **Treatment:** five excerpts in printed reading order (`history.software-listing-1988`), each keeping its heading, platform qualifier, catalog number, and price: Color Computer DeskMate, DeskMate II for PC-compatible/2000, and Color Computer 3 DeskMate 3 on p. 102; Personal DeskMate, Personal DeskMate 2, and Professional DeskMate on p. 103. Personal DeskMate 2 flows from the foot of p. 103's left column to the head of its right column, so it is two panels of one entry, not two products. Unrelated Offix, Open Access, PFS, GEM, and Framework entries are excluded. **Proposed caption:** “Radio Shack offered several distinct DeskMate products in its 1988 catalog. The separately listed Color Computer DeskMate 3 belonged to a different platform family.” **Alt text:** “Catalog clippings of Tandy's 1988 software listings: Color Computer DeskMate, DeskMate II, Color Computer 3 DeskMate 3, Personal DeskMate, Personal DeskMate 2, and Professional DeskMate, with prices and supported computer families.” **Status:** both page images inspected; crops prepared for private review; publication pending the rights decision. Avoid an isolated crop of ‘DeskMate 3’ without its Color Computer qualifier.

3. **DeskMate actually in ROM — original 1988 Tandy 1000 SL advertisement.** Source: [*COMPUTE!*, October 1988, full issue](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Magazines/Compute%20%28Clean%29/Compute_Issue_101_1988_Oct.pdf), advertisement headed “The New Tandy 1000 SL,” PDF p. 27, printed p. 25 (an unnumbered full-page advertisement between printed pp. 24 and 26). **Placement:** centerpiece of the 1988 ROM-generation passage, visually contrasted with figure 1. **Treatment:** the advertisement's headline claim, machine photograph, and opening copy, which states that the 1000 SL features MS-DOS and DeskMate in ROM (`history.sl-rom-ad-1988`); the rest of the page, including the catalogue-request coupon, is omitted. **Proposed caption:** “In 1988, Tandy advertised the 1000 SL with both MS-DOS and DeskMate in ROM—a distinct arrangement from the HX's DOS-in-ROM bundle.” **Alt text:** “Period Radio Shack advertisement headed ‘The New Tandy 1000 SL,’ with the claim ‘Built-in MS-DOS and DeskMate for the ultimate in ease of use,’ a photograph of the computer, and body copy describing MS-DOS and DeskMate in ROM.” **Status:** page located and visually inspected; the printed advertisement makes no shipment-date claim, so the chronology assigns it to 1988 as advertising, not as evidence of a launch day. Publication pending the rights decision.

4. **What a buyer took home — original Personal DeskMate 2 documentation.** Source: [photograph in the vintagecomputer.net Tandy 1000 TX collection](https://www.vintagecomputer.net/tandy/1000_tx/Tandy_25-1600_sn-511576_docs2.jpg) and [context page](https://www.vintagecomputer.net/tandy/1000_tx/). **Placement:** near the early bundle narrative as a smaller contextual illustration. **Treatment:** attributed external link only. The site states “All Images Copyright Degnan, Co. 1949 – 2026”, and no permission has been sought, so the photograph is **not** prepared as a local derivative (`history.pdm2-manuals-photo`). **Proposed caption:** “A surviving Personal DeskMate 2 manual beside a Tandy 1000 TX guide, photographed in a modern collection.” **Alt text:** “Two spiral-bound Tandy manuals photographed side by side, including the Personal DeskMate 2 cover.” **Status:** photograph inspected and credited; no reproduction basis recorded.

**Hold for the second pass:** individual RSX installation disks, temporary seller listings, full packaging spreads, unverified 1989 catalog page crops, and supplementary TL advertisements. They are valuable for individual release articles once exact media/version identities and image provenance are established. This is a deliberate editorial selection, not a claim that those sources are unimportant.

Page conventions differ between the two scans and are recorded per source rather than assumed:

- **1988 catalog:** printed page = zero-based PDF page index + 1. Printed pp. 9 and 10 carry their folios, as do pp. 102 and 103. The catalog bears a 1988 cover date and a 1987 copyright notice in its introductory matter; cite it as the **1988 catalog edition**, not as an assertion that every passage was written in 1988.
- **COMPUTE! October 1988:** printed page = zero-based PDF page index − 1, checked against printed folios on the surrounding pages (PDF p. 25 is printed p. 23; PDF p. 32 is printed p. 30). The Tandy advertisement is PDF p. 27 and printed p. 25, a full-page advertisement with no folio of its own. The same issue's news section reports Tandy's 27 July 1988 announcement of the SL and TL and lists the SL at $899; the advertisement adds the ROM claim but no availability date.

## Private figure preparation

[`tools/prepare_history_figures.py`](../../tools/prepare_history_figures.py) reads the single reviewed record [`history-figure-sources.json`](history-figure-sources.json) and refuses anything that does not match it: an unexpected source digest, byte size, or page count, a page whose extracted text lacks the expected product names, a rectangle outside the page box or with no area, an output that exceeds the recorded dimensions, pixel count, or byte cap, and a figure record missing a caption, alternative text, attribution, purpose, or rights status.

Crop rectangles are PDF points measured from the top-left corner of the unrotated page box, in `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` order. A page is rendered once at the recorded resolution and cropped with `round(point × dpi / 72)`, so the same sources and a compatible Pillow produce the same dimensions and pixels. Multi-panel figures are stacked in printed reading order on a white canvas with a recorded gap; the manifest records each panel's page index, printed page, rectangle, pixel box, and vertical offset.

To reproduce the private review package:

```bash
python -m venv /tmp/history-figures-env
/tmp/history-figures-env/bin/pip install "pymupdf>=1.24,<2" "Pillow>=13,<14"
python tools/prepare_history_figures.py --record-only
python tools/prepare_history_figures.py \
  --output /tmp/deskmate-history-figures \
  --source tandy-1988-catalog=/path/to/1988-tandy-catalog.pdf \
  --source compute-1988-10=/path/to/compute-1988-10.pdf
```

The output directory must stay outside tracked project content; `local-results/` and `private-fixtures/` are the only in-repository locations the tool will write to. The run produces the three PNG crops, a `contact-sheet.png` pairing each crop with its proposed caption and rights status, and `figure-manifest.json`, which records each image's digest, size, dimensions, panels, source digest, caption, alternative text, attribution, and rights status. Source PDFs and all of these files stay private. The synthetic regression suite in [`tests/portable/test_prepare_history_figures.py`](../../tests/portable/test_prepare_history_figures.py) covers page selection, coordinate conversion, compositing, output limits, record rejection, and manifest provenance without any historical source.

## Editorial priorities

First, illustrate the contrast between **DeskMate bundled with a machine** and **DeskMate in ROM**: the 1988 HX catalog page versus the 1988 SL advertisement and 1989 SL/TL catalog pages. Second, illustrate the variety of retail releases using the 1988 product pages. Third, add high-quality photographs of original disks, manuals, and packaging alongside the version histories emerging from [installation Issue #153](https://github.com/smartyhall/deskmate-formats/issues/153).

Record, for every chosen item, its source URL, catalog number, printed and PDF page, software and hardware edition, original work credit, scan or photographer credit, evidence confidence, publication review, caption, and alt text. Keep original facsimile links alongside any permitted excerpt or crop. Apply our Oxford-comma rule to public captions.

## Recorded limits and open questions

- The 1988 catalog and the *COMPUTE!* issue are third-party commercial publications. No permission or licence has been located, and none has been requested. [The rights assessment](figure-rights-assessment.md) sets out the options; the operator's decision is the remaining publication gate.
- The 1984 catalog link cited by the chronology (`device.report`) was unavailable during the earlier review and was not re-checked in this pass.
- The 1989 RSC-20 listings remain text-verified only; their page images have not been inspected, so no crop is specified.
- The catalog scan's OCR layer is noisy. Every product name, catalog number, price, and page number used in a caption was checked against the rendered page image, not against the extracted text; the tool's extracted-text check is a guard against selecting the wrong leaf, not a citation source.
- Crop rectangles describe this scan's page geometry. A different scan of the same publication is a different source and needs its own record.
