# Period images and artifact sources for the DeskMate timeline

*Editorial sourcing record, 2026-11-21. Candidate historical images are not yet approved website assets.*

The timeline should show **how DeskMate was marketed, packaged, and supplied**, not only modern captures of the software. Prioritize original Tandy and Radio Shack catalog scans and contemporary magazine advertisements. For artifact photographs, retain credit for the modern photographer or collection. Never imply a modern photograph is itself a period image, or that a picture of an original disk verifies its contents.

1. **The computer with software included — 1988 Tandy 1000 HX catalog page.** Source: [1988 Tandy Computer Catalog, p. 9](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Catalogs/Radio%20Shack/Tandy%20Computer%20Catalog%20and%20Software%20Reference%20Guide%20%281988%29%28Tandy%29.pdf#page=9). **Placement:** alongside the Personal DeskMate 2 / HX/TX passage. **Treatment:** reproduce a legible full-page excerpt, or a photograph-and-offer crop accompanied by a link to the whole leaf. **Proposed caption:** “The 1988 Tandy catalog advertised the 1000 HX with Personal DeskMate 2 included. Its built-in ROM contained MS-DOS, not an explicitly advertised copy of DeskMate.” **Alt text:** “1988 Tandy 1000 HX catalog listing with a photograph of the computer, a $699 price, and separate claims for MS-DOS in ROM and included Personal DeskMate 2.” **Status:** original catalog page visually inspected.

2. **The range of DeskMate editions — 1988 software catalog listings.** Source: [1988 Tandy Computer Catalog, pp. 102–103](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Catalogs/Radio%20Shack/Tandy%20Computer%20Catalog%20and%20Software%20Reference%20Guide%20%281988%29%28Tandy%29.pdf#page=102). **Placement:** at the transition from the early bundled products to DeskMate 3, paired with a note on independently marketed software. **Treatment:** readable, separately reviewed crops of the p. 102 DeskMate II retail entry and Color Computer entries, and the p. 103 entries in its two-column layout for Personal DeskMate, Personal DeskMate 2, and Professional DeskMate. Preserve platform qualifiers and both original full-page links. The p. 10 SX bundle is separate supporting evidence. **Proposed caption:** “Radio Shack offered several distinct DeskMate products in its 1988 catalog. The separately listed Color Computer DeskMate 3 belonged to a different platform family.” **Alt text:** “Tandy's 1988 software listings show multiple named DeskMate editions, prices, and supported computer families.” **Status:** page images inspected. Avoid an isolated crop of ‘DeskMate 3’ without its Color Computer qualifier.

3. **DeskMate actually in ROM — original 1988 Tandy 1000 SL advertisement.** Source: [*COMPUTE!*, October 1988, full issue](https://colorcomputerarchive.com/repo/Documents/Magazines/Compute%20%28Clean%29/Compute_Issue_101_1988_Oct.pdf), advertisement headed “The New Tandy 1000 SL.” **Placement:** centerpiece of the 1988 ROM-generation passage, visually contrasted with figure 1. **Treatment:** original advertisement with the prominently printed built-in MS-DOS/DeskMate claim legible; link to its full magazine source. **Proposed caption:** “In 1988, Tandy advertised the 1000 SL with both MS-DOS and DeskMate in ROM—a distinct arrangement from the HX's DOS-in-ROM bundle.” **Alt text:** “Period Radio Shack advertisement showing the Tandy 1000 SL and promoting built-in MS-DOS and DeskMate.” **Status:** period advertisement wording independently checked; the original PDF is too large for the available page-screenshot renderer, so **the exact scan page and derivative still require direct visual proof before publication**.

4. **What a buyer took home — original Personal DeskMate 2 documentation.** Source: [photograph in the vintagecomputer.net Tandy 1000 TX collection](https://www.vintagecomputer.net/tandy/1000_tx/Tandy_25-1600_sn-511576_docs2.jpg) and [context page](https://www.vintagecomputer.net/tandy/1000_tx/). **Placement:** near the early bundle narrative as a smaller contextual illustration. **Treatment:** original photograph as a linked reference initially; if reproducing locally, obtain/record the relevant image reuse basis and credit the photographer or collection. **Proposed caption:** “A surviving Personal DeskMate 2 manual beside a Tandy 1000 TX guide, photographed in a modern collection.” **Alt text:** “Two spiral-bound Tandy manuals photographed side by side, including the Personal DeskMate 2 cover.” **Status:** object and photo visually identified; image rights and stable publication derivative not yet checked.

**Hold for the second pass:** individual RSX installation disks, temporary seller listings, full packaging spreads, unverified 1989 catalog page crops, and supplementary TL advertisements. They are valuable for individual release articles once exact media/version identities and image provenance are established. This is a deliberate editorial selection, not a claim that those sources are unimportant.
