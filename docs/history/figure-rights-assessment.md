# Publication-rights assessment: period figures for the DeskMate chronology

*Prepared for operator review, 27 November 2026. No historical image is published, committed, or linked from the website by this assessment.*

[PR #155](https://github.com/smartyhall/deskmate-formats/pull/155) proposes four period figures for the chronology. Three would be local crops from commercial publications; the fourth is a modern photograph that stays an external link. This document records, for each one, who holds the original rights, where the copy came from, what permission exists, why the excerpt is proposed, and what remains unresolved.

## The standard being applied

[LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md) accepts selected archival material — period advertisements, catalog excerpts, packaging, and manual illustrations — for identification, documentation, comparison, and commentary, with attribution and source links, where a permission, licence, public-domain status, or a warranted fair-use basis applies. It also states that the educational or noncommercial character of the project does **not** automatically establish that a reproduction qualifies, and asks that limited, relevant excerpts and links be preferred over full-document reproductions.

The operator's standing documentary-image grant of 21 November 2026 covers ordinary DeskMate interface captures made with synthetic or public sample content, the project's own synthetic illustrations, and suitably small figures prepared for public documentation. It explicitly does **not** extend to "complete manuals or archives" or to commercial advertising, and it conveys no rights in third-party artwork.

Two assumptions are therefore rejected here: that a 1988 publication is old enough to be free to reuse, and that availability on an archive site implies permission. The Color Computer Archive and VintageComputer.net provide access and hosting; neither is a rights grant.

## Recommendation for the operator

1. **Default, no additional risk:** publish the chronology with figure 4 linked and figures 1–3 represented by their original source links, as the draft does today. This needs no new rights judgement.
2. **Limited-excerpt publication:** if the operator accepts a limited-excerpt basis for figures 1–3, publish only the prepared crops, each with the publisher attribution, a rights note stating that the original rights are retained and that the project claims no licence in the work, a link to the complete original, and a caption that identifies and comments on the product rather than reproducing the leaf for its own sake.
3. **Either way:** do not commit source PDFs, do not publish whole pages or whole issues, do not present an excerpt as a facsimile, and keep the timeline out of site navigation until the decision is recorded.

A permission from the current owner of the Tandy/Radio Shack catalog and advertising material, a documented public-domain determination, or a licence from the scan repository would each change this assessment. None has been sought; this document records the absence rather than assuming it away.

## What the project license does and does not cover

The project's GPL and CC offers cover the crop selection, captions, alternative text, provenance records, and the preparation tool. They do not relicense the scanned catalog pages, the advertisement, the photograph, or any Tandy or COMPUTE! expression within them. A published figure's REUSE sidecar must say so explicitly.
