This does *not* establish OS confinement or permit arbitrary actions. Read [the experimental-lab posture](experimental-lab-operating-posture.md) and [standing instructions](../../AGENTS.md).
- GitHub is the **review and handoff surface**; private queue databases, exact execution plans, credentials, native raw evidence, full model responses, and VM snapshots remain in protected local laboratory storage, not public Git. Do not invent access to those paths from a new chat.

Preserve exact source identity, operation ledgers, permanent no-replay-on-uncertainty behavior, ordinary Windows 10 compatibility, private evidence, publication audits and correct attribution of experimental results.
