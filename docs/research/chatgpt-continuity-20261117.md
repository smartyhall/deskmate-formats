This does *not* establish OS confinement or permit arbitrary actions. Read [the experimental-lab posture](experimental-lab-operating-posture.md) and [standing instructions](../../AGENTS.md).
- GitHub is the **review and handoff surface**; private queue databases, exact execution plans, credentials, native raw evidence, full model responses, and VM snapshots remain in protected local laboratory storage, not public Git. Do not invent access to those paths from a new chat.
- The typical loop is: ChatGPT drafts a narrowly bounded, paste-ready prompt for a manually operated Codex session; Codex implements on a branch and opens a PR; ChatGPT independently examines the implementation, evidence and latest-head Windows/Ubuntu CI, asks for targeted fixes when necessary, and may merge after review. `AGENTS.md` records standing authority for ChatGPT to merge ready reviewed Codex PRs. **Codex workers may not self-merge.** No standing authorization exists for new paid/native model turns, emulator operations, automatic acceptance/dispatch, or changes to host security.
