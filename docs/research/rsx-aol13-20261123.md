# Tandy OEM America Online 1.3 on the Tandy 1000 RSX

November 23, 2026.

The original America Online 1.3 installer copied the Tandy OEM edition to
`C:\AOL` under Tandy MS-DOS 5.00 on an emulated 1000 RSX. With the installed
DOS startup configuration active and A: empty, the original DeskMate
03.04.04 AOL shortcut opened the GEOS first-run display setup. The source
floppy has the label `TANDYAOL`. The installed hard disk has the label
`MS-DOS_5`. The original DeskMate installation without AOL had prompted for
a disk named `AOL`. [Earlier RSX recovery](deskmate-rsx-oem-recovery-20261122.md#other-rsx-applications)

## Media and installation

The locally preserved `disk01.img` matches the [WinWorld Tandy OEM listing](https://winworldpc.com/product/aol/10)
and the previously inspected SHA-256
`41a14d087306f3359679352b2d161638f1243480a3896cbdb35482168ce4acc7`.
The 1,474,560-byte image has a FAT12 filesystem, 512-byte sectors and
clusters, and four files. `VERSION.ID` and `INSTALL.INI` identify version
1.3. The 847,724-byte `AOL1.BIN` is an embedded PKWARE ZIP with a 12,784-byte
stub, 80 Implode entries (method 6), and three stored entries. The 83 entries
total 1,381,295 expanded bytes. 7-Zip 26.05 extracted all entries for offline
comparison. The earlier RSX survey reported that the separate mirrored DOS
1.6 distribution lacks this `KERNEL.EXE`. This trial used the hash-pinned
WinWorld 1.3 image throughout. [Media and installed-file inventory](../evidence/rsx-aol13-20261123.json)

The writable RSX clone came from the previous clean OEM install with SHA-256
`3085c68955b7fcad648995a40ff30f8f3a799e943710bd43cc0326c69a107fc1`.
Guest input and observations used the visible automation build of 86Box
through its authenticated native loopback socket. The guest had a 25 MHz
386SX, 4 MiB RAM, internal VGA, a PS/2 mouse, a 1.44 MB A: drive, and the
original IDE geometry of 1,024 cylinders, four heads, and 26 sectors. The
profile had network links down and no external serial backend or modem.

Boot with A: empty, confirm DOS with `VER`, mount a copy of the floppy,
and run `A:\INSTALL` from A:. Answer No to prior AOL and No to GeoWorks
Ensemble, acknowledge C: as the available drive, and accept `\AOL`.
Use Make All Suggested Changes, and accept adding AOL to the DOS search
path. The installer increases `FILES` and `BUFFERS` to 30, saves
`CONFIG.OLD` and `AUTOEXEC.OLD`, and appends `C:\AOL` to `PATH`.
The original `SET DMCONFIG=C:\DESKMATE` stays in place. The copy finished
between 115,534 and 137,534 emulated ms from power-on. The completion screen
was captured at 142,850 ms. Enter entered GEOS first-run setup by
147,854 ms.

The initial `GEOS.INI` activates VGA 640 × 480 in 16 colors. The native
framebuffer measured BIOS mode `0x12` at 640 × 480 throughout graphical
setup and client use. Setup offered F10 to choose a different display,
including 800 × 600 Super VGA options. It drew alignment markers and
16 color swatches on the tested VGA path. The inventory also identifies
bundled EGA, CGA, HGC, MCGA, and AT&T 6300 drivers. MS-DOS 5.00 with HIMEM
worked on this machine. DOS 2/3/4 and DR DOS drivers are present in the
payload. Minimum DOS version, minimum conventional or extended memory,
and operation of the other video drivers were unmeasured.

## Reproduce the offline launch

1. Copy the clean RSX hard disk, config, and NVRAM into a new machine
   directory. Point the cloned config at the writable hard disk. Keep
   guest networking down and leave the UARTs without a host modem, serial
   passthrough, or network backend. [Visible launcher and socket client](86box-visible-operation.md#preferred-launch-procedure)
2. Launch the profile with `python tools/86box_launch.py --machine <clone>`.
   Use the [socket client](86box-client.md#supported-operations) against the
   same directory for held status, advancement, floppy mounts, keyboard,
   and capture. The tested launcher used seed 1, source revision
   `855258da966f1594f568350be59e8e1eca082ed1`, and 86Box 7.0 executable SHA-256
   `e7e1244588ea721aa239564a43fa2636611ae965ea8431672fdc06d8c12c280d`.
   Use the RSX BIOS, ROM revision, and DOS configuration from the pinned
   baseline. [ROM and build identities](deskmate-303-305-comparison.md#installation-and-controls)
3. Advance the boot by 35,000 emulated ms with A: empty, then use the
   native installer sequence above. In graphical setup, accept the detected
   VGA mode and the alignment and color tests. At mouse selection, type
   I to reach IBM PS/2 Mouse. Use socket-relative motion and left button
   press/release over **Click here to test**. The guest accepted that driver.
   The pilot reached the COM and baud pages (COM1 and 2400 selected).
   Navigation stalled or went back there in the tested walkthrough, so
   the native completion sequence for the full setup wizard remains open.
4. Preserve the installed disk before the offline control. Stop through
   the socket, preserve the stopped image, eject the installer floppy, and
   reboot the clone. In the installed `C:\AOL\GEOS.INI`, change only
   `continueSetup = true` to `continueSetup = false` under `[system]`.
   The trial transferred the edited copy on a temporary FAT12 control disk,
   renamed the original to `GEOS.NAT`, copied the replacement, and ejected
   the control disk. This leaves the native installer and installed
   executable bytes intact. The retained `[mouse]` section identifies
   `IBM PS/2 Mouse`, `PS2.GEO`, and `info = 0`.
5. From the DOS prompt at C:\, run `C:\AOL\AOL.BAT`. The generated batch
   switches to C:, changes to `\AOL`, and runs `KERNEL`. Another entry is
   `C:`, `CD \AOL`, and `KERNEL`. The shipped `GEOS.BAT` invokes
   `KERNEL` from the caller's current directory.
6. The first client dialog offers to confirm telephone setup. Click
   **Cancel** to reach the **Welcome to America Online!** window. Leave
   **Sign On**, telephone confirmation, and all external service commands
   unused. F3 exits AOL. A standalone invocation returned to the DOS
   prompt in `C:\AOL`. A DeskMate invocation returned to the Desktop.

The first stopped post-install image was captured after 220,494 emulated ms
in session 1. The second session's captures start from its own cold boot.
Displayed guest date differs from the campaign date because the baseline
uses a pinned, unsynchronized guest clock.

## Original DeskMate shortcut

Start `DESK` from `C:\DESKMATE`. On the stock Desktop, Tab five times
from Teach Me to reach AOL, or click the label and confirm the highlight.
Press Enter. The clean Desktop's four root and DeskMate-directory
`DEFAULT.CFG` / `DESKTOP.CFG` copies contain the same relevant byte
sequence. At offset `0x587` it contains the NUL-padded field `AOL`, and
at `0x592` it contains `KERNEL.EXE`. No `AOL.BAT` is named in that record.

The Tandy OEM 1.3 installation is a working companion for the shortcut.
The observed hard-disk route accepts the installed directory on the
`MS-DOS_5` volume without a floppy or a PATH entry for AOL. In the
tested missing-target state, the wording `AOL` describes the media
requested by the fallback. A matching label alone cannot provide the
expected executable. The relabel control changed the 11-byte label
fields at boot offset 43 and FAT root offset 9728 on a clone. The
archive and files remained unchanged. The exact precedence of current
directory, installation directory, PATH, and floppy search has not
been reconstructed.

The process probe was an independently authored DOS MZ program. It
recorded DOS interrupt 21h functions 19h and 47h, and the PSP command
tail at offset `0x80`, in a 196-byte receipt on C:. A direct DOS
control called the probe with `HELLO` and recorded the leading
space and argument. The Desktop launch recorded an empty tail.
The end-user file picker of the real kernel starts in `C:\AOL`
even though the probe measured entry with `C:\DESKMATE` current.
Directly running `C:\AOL\KERNEL.EXE` from `C:\DESKMATE` also opened
the client and returned to that DOS directory. The working-directory
switch is attributable to startup after the DeskMate handoff. The two
synthetic floppy probes did not establish the fallback lookup sequence.
Both entered blank text mode during
their observation windows, and neither produced a verified new receipt.

## Offline applications and storage

The top-level menus are Help, File, Edit, Go To, Mail, Members, and Window.
The Welcome panel presents a screen-name selector, Sign On, Setup, and Help.
File exposes New, Open, Save, Save As, Disk Utilities, Print, Logging,
Cancel Action, and Exit. In the signed-out session, the Mail menu offered
Compose Mail and Edit Address Book. Read New Mail, previously read or sent
mail, and Fax/Paper Mail displayed disabled. Go To had enabled Network News
and Edit Go To Menu entries alongside disabled service sections. Menu
enablement documents the screen state. Mail delivery, any server-supplied
views, and later client releases were outside the measured workflow.

**Mail → Edit Address Book** opened offline. The stock list held an
America Online Customer Service group. Create accepted a synthetic
group, `RSX Lab Group`, and a screen-name string, `RSXLabTest`. The
group reopened at 98,715 emulated ms in a fresh client process
launched by the original DeskMate shortcut. The address-book entry
is stored in `SYSTEM/QFORMS/PCAODB.VM`. Between the immediately
preceding offline snapshot and the restored final disk, this file
grew from 32,160 to 34,544 bytes. It was the only AOL file whose
bytes changed after accounting for restoring the kernel under its
original name. The two synthetic strings appear at zero-based offsets
34,259 and 6,782 respectively. The VM container also holds shipped
form data, so the mixed file stays private. [Byte identities and capture provenance](../evidence/rsx-aol13-20261123.json)

The standalone DOS trial created a File → New document, typed two
synthetic lines, and saved it in the default dialog directory as
`C:\AOL\RSXNOTE.TXT`. A fresh session opened from the DeskMate shortcut
reopened both lines with File → Open. Independent extraction of the stopped
disk yielded 35 ASCII bytes, with DOS CRLF between lines and no terminal
newline. The independent FAT walker and 7-Zip agreed on the exact bytes.
This specimen uses plain text. [Native fixture](../../tests/fixtures/application-generated/aol/rsxnote.txt)

The native install and first partial setup left 90 files under `C:\AOL`.
All 83 archive paths were present. 82 matched byte-for-byte, `GEOS.INI`
changed, and seven extra files appeared. The `.GEO` and observed `.VM`
headers share the `C7 45 CF 53` signature. The executable and resource
roles above follow from their bytes, configuration, and the launch
observations, without a complete import or VM block decoder. The stock
DeskMate shortcut hands off to a DOS executable. Its own DeskMate
applications use PDM modules and separately observed DeskMate storage
formats. The mechanism provides a substantive boundary between the two
runtimes.

The next useful archival tests would compare snapshots around
individual Help and address-book edit or delete actions, and parse the GEOS
geode and VM headers against measured sample identities. A complete
GeoWorks installation was unnecessary on the tested path. Broad
interoperability with other AOL releases and other PC/GEOS installations
remains open.

## Evidence and validation

The [structured inventory](../evidence/rsx-aol13-20261123.json) records
media and archive-member identities, post-install and offline file deltas,
and frame timings and hashes. The [Evolution Atlas](../ecosystem/deskmate-evolution-atlas.md#america-online-on-the-rsx)
indexes eight representative frames from the native framebuffer, re-encoded
at the original size without changing RGB pixels. Original media, raw
captures, machine images, and the commercial payload remain in private lab
storage. The publication test pins the byte-exact synthetic note, rejects
line-ending normalization, and restricts manifest eligibility to its
reviewed path in the AOL fixture directory.

The final working image was stopped normally at the DOS prompt with A:
empty and the original kernel restored. Its SHA-256 is
`86ebd863c0e295afe606f5f7ed3e97062b773ff6ac04415ff5b7cea87d5f4da1`.
The source floppy rehashed to the original identity after the trials.

The 28 repository audit tests, 17 native socket client tests, and 18
website tests passed. The reference preview built 36 HTML pages with zero
internal link check errors. The test data and original media were absent
from the wheel and sdist.
