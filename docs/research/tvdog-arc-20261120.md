# ARC extraction and the TVDog residual population

The settled index held 48 partial analyses. ARC accounted for 34, ZIP
for eight, and MZ/SFX containers for six. The ARC failures were 123
members with method 8 and 14 with method 9. The native header reader
already knew their ordinals, names, sizes and CRC fields.

The optional decoder is Russell Marks's nomarch 1.4 (GPL-2.0-or-later).
The lab built it with MSYS2 UCRT64 GCC 16.2.0 (Rev6) from the Debian
upstream tarball, SHA-256
`fe20da34e0d3ba0cf6388701f44ac22224cf65130ddbc5fcbc27bc4949a6e1ad`.
The private Windows build used an entry wrapper that calls
`_setmode(_fileno(stdout), _O_BINARY)` before the upstream main routine.
The resulting SHA-256 is
`66f972f7503121f1b65b5ef68046c19dd07b469a90bfda9ee2297bbbf679a5ad`.
The build emitted a compiler warning in the Huffman decoder. This
adapter exercises methods 8 and 9.

The adapter invokes `nomarch -p` on a temporary archive containing one
exact header and packed payload plus the ARC terminator. This avoids
wildcard selection and preserves duplicate-name ordinals. It verifies
output length and CRC within the archive's time and byte budgets. With
the optional decoder omitted, methods 8 and 9 remain unsupported.

All 34 previously partial ARC identities were replayed privately. All
138 members passed CRC and length checks, including one stored member
already recovered. The 137 newly recovered members total 1,742,809
bytes. A private copy of the settled index used `--update-tools` to
reindex five affected roots. Total hashed occurrences increased from
24,915 to 25,052 and distinct digests from 20,244 to 20,360. The 9,973
verified originals were retained. The result has 14 partial, seven
limited and three failed analyses, plus 22 member read errors.

The receipts, `arc-eval.sqlite` and compiler inputs are under the private
`extended-20261120` evidence root. The portable test uses a synthetic
single-member header and injected decoder output to exercise bounded
selection and CRC rejection. The actual compressed payload coverage
comes from the measured private corpus run.
