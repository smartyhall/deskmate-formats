Verify that historical and format claims are limited to cited evidence,
named releases and actual observations. Distinguish vendor claims,
contemporary sources, synthetic tests, native experiments and
hypotheses. Do not invent citations or require an external source for
a properly cited project experiment. Enforce Oxford commas only in
public-facing editorial prose, never in quotations or raw evidence.
